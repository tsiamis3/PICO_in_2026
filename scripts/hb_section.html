What it shows

  • A1 and A3: σnetwork is calibrated. The core scale is λ0 ≈ 0.92 with ν ≈ 12–15, so the total width λ0·√(ν/(ν−2)) ≈ 1.00 σnetwork. λ varies only ~2–5% with charge and position (τu, τv ≈ 0.02–0.06), and the network’s ±1σ / ±2σ intervals already hold 69% / 95% of the events.
  • A2: σnetwork underestimates the error, mostly at low charge. λ goes from ≈ 1.67 at Q ≈ 27 to ≈ 0.9 at Q ≈ 99, with very heavy tails (ν ≈ 2.8). The bias goes from about −12 ps to +11 ps across the charge range. The network’s ±1σ holds only 44% of the events; with λiσi and the Student-t the coverage goes back to 68% / 96%. Retraining on noisy data (A3) removes both effects.
  • Position-dependent bias in every scenario. τb ≈ 3 ps (A1, A3) and ≈ 4.7 ps (A2). The largest part is a narrow band at x ≈ 0.4–0.5 cm, along the boundary between the centre and the x > 0 pads, at about +8 to +12 ps. There is no such band at x ≈ −0.5 cm. It appears in A1 and in A3, so it comes from the data, not from one training. In A2, λ is also largest along the pad boundaries (x ≈ ±0.5 cm and y ≈ 0), where the charge is shared between pads.
  • Pooling. Every cell has at least a few hundred events, so pooling changes the cell values only a little. The hierarchical λ per cell is also conditional on charge (the charge dependence is a separate term). The independent fit mixes the charge spectrum of the cell, so the two do not lie exactly on the diagonal.
  • Sampling. 0 divergences. Largest R̂ 1.02 (A1), 1.045 (A2), 1.015 (A3). For the quantities in the plots it is 1.02, 1.036 and 1.011. A2 mixes the slowest (ESS ≈ 50 for the plotted values), so read its curves as ± a few percent.
